# Online TV: user sources and rights

DS Gallery is a player, not a broadcaster, subscription service or source directory.
It supplies no channel URLs or playlists. Users may add their own HTTPS M3U/M3U8
playlist URLs or individual streams. Include only official, trusted sources you
are authorized to access in your country. A public URL or a free playlist does
not establish permission. No broadcaster rights or trademark permissions are granted.

The application does not remove DRM, bypass geoblocking, sell access or relay
streams. First-use notices explain authorized use and external requests; they
are not waivers of mandatory rights or the developer’s own obligations.

Do not post account passwords, token-bearing stream URLs or personal data in
public issues. User-provided
sources remain under the user’s control; examples must use authorized material.
