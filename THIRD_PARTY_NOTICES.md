GIF frame decoder
-----------------

The standalone Glide GIF decoder 4.16.0 is used for current-frame counting and frame selection. Glide is distributed under BSD-style, Apache 2.0 and MIT terms; its GIF decoder retains the MIT notice for Xcellent Creations, Inc. (2013). Source and license notices: https://github.com/bumptech/glide/tree/v4.16.0/third_party/gif_decoder and https://github.com/bumptech/glide/blob/v4.16.0/LICENSE. The ordinary image pipeline remains Coil.

Every distributed APK is accompanied by its matching [LGPL reconstruction kit](docs/LGPL_RELINKING.md), including the actual jump3r source archive and application build sources. The kit permits replacing that library and rebuilding without maintainer signing keys.
